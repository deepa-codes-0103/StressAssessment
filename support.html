Support & Assistance

Based on your assessment, here are some support options for you.

Professional Support

You can speak with a trained support professional who can listen to your concerns and help you decide what to do next.

Immediate Help

If you feel unsafe or believe you are in immediate danger, seek help from a trusted person or the appropriate emergency service.

Self-Care & Coping

Take a few slow breaths, move to a safe and quiet place, and consider talking to someone you trust.

You do not have to deal with difficult feelings alone.

Immediate Help

If you feel unsafe or need urgent assistance, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Continue Later

You can leave this page and return to the portal whenever you are ready.

Your privacy and safety are important to us.

This platform is a support tool and does not replace professional evaluation.
