AI-Based Stress & Trauma Assessment

A safe and confidential platform to help assess stress and trauma indicators and connect you with appropriate support.

How does it work?

1. Answer a few simple questions.

2. Our system analyzes your responses.

3. You receive an assessment result.

4. You can explore suitable support options.

Your privacy and safety are important to us.
