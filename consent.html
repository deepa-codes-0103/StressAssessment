Before You Begin

This assessment will ask you a few questions about your current feelings and experiences.

Your answers will be used to identify stress and trauma-related indicators.

Your information is sensitive and should be handled with care.

Important Information

This assessment is only a support tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis.

You can stop the assessment at any time.

Your privacy and safety are important to us.
